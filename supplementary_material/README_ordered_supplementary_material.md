## Suggested manuscript wording

Supplementary material includes: Supplementary File S1, selected comparator drug dictionary; S2, depression-indication dictionary; S3, narrow sexual-dysfunction PT dictionary; S4, broad sexual-dysfunction PT dictionary; S5, complete primary 2 x 2 pairwise case/non-case tables; S6, PRR and chi-square robustness table; S7, READUS-PV checklist; S8, analysis commands and reproducibility log. The broader all-antidepressant analysis is reported only as contextual supplementary material. The revised supplementary package also includes concomitant drug and condition flag dictionaries, flag counts, confounder-exclusion 2 x 2 tables, and stratified analyses for the primary comparison.

## Data cut-off

The public FAERS/AEMS quarterly ASCII files covered 2014 Q1 through 2025 Q4. The download/extraction date recorded in the revised manuscript is 31 March 2026, and 2025 Q4 was the prespecified analytic cut-off.
