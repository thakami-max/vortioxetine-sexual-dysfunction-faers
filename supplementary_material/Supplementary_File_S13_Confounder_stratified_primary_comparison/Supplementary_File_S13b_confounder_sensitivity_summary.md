# Concomitant drug/condition sensitivity analysis summary

Final selected exposure rows loaded: 56,051
Unique selected cases loaded: 56,051
Sexual-dysfunction cases in selected cohort: 1,656

Core confounder flags excluding PDE5/ED treatment were identified in 10,964 cases. Any prespecified confounder excluding PDE5/ED treatment was identified in 16,443 cases, and any prespecified confounder including PDE5/ED treatment was identified in 16,479 cases. PDE5/ED treatment co-reporting was flagged separately because it may represent treatment of the reported outcome rather than baseline confounding.

The primary vortioxetine-versus-escitalopram estimate remained lower with vortioxetine after excluding core confounders except PDE5/ED treatment: ROR 0.44 (95% CI 0.36-0.54). Similar findings were obtained after excluding any prespecified confounder except PDE5/ED treatment (ROR 0.40, 95% CI 0.33-0.49) and after additionally excluding PDE5/ED treatment co-reporting (ROR 0.40, 95% CI 0.33-0.49).

These analyses are sensitivity analyses based on observable drug and indication/reporting fields in public FAERS/AEMS extracts. They cannot remove unmeasured confounding.
